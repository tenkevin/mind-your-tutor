# QA criteria: C9 · Apply D12 and D13

Done when: contracts accept empty `Units.objective` and `Assessment.question`; C1 imports them without skipping or blocking; C2 flags them; the built-in personas live only in `packages/ai`; a pack with no Personas tab stores none; the resolved C1 questions about required fields and built-in personas are removed by subject (current unrelated question numbers are preserved).
Sources: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` D9, D12 and D13, `docs/spec/curriculum-pack.fields.json` ("missing" behaviour), `docs/qa/C1.md`, `docs/qa/C2.md`, `docs/qa/F3.md`, `fixtures/water-cycle`.
