# QA criteria: S3 · Teacher-assigned study track (missions) from due dates

Done when: per D39, the teacher-assigned track lists work from teacher due dates and assignments and always takes priority over the self-paced track in the student UI.
Sources: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` D39 (dual-track plans; teacher always first), §6 (due dates per class), §8.2 (`mission.completed`); T4 missions feed; S2; S5 (choice and pacing, D17); A12 builds the self-paced track. *Revised 2026-10-29 for D39 before any claim; S3-F6 added.*

## Functional

| Id | Weight | Check | Steps | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S3-F1 | must | Uses T4 dates | The teacher sets dates for units 1 and 3 | Missions list units 1 and 3 by date, then undated units |
| S3-F2 | must | Live update | The teacher moves a date | The list reorders on next load |
| S3-F3 | must | Completion | Finish a unit (all ideas mastered and teach-back passed) | `mission.completed` is sent once; the mission shows as done |
| S3-F4 | must | Choice (D17) | The student picks any available mission | Allowed; due dates guide, they don't lock |
| S3-F6 | must | Teacher track first (D39) | Give the student a teacher-assigned unit with a due date and some self-paced items (seed them directly if A12 hasn't merged) | Teacher-assigned work is listed first and marked as from the teacher; self-paced items never appear above it or hide its due date; with the class's self-paced track turned off, only teacher work shows |
| S3-F5 | must | Everything passes | Root lint, format:check, typecheck, test with DATABASE_URL (including `function_binding.test.ts`), build, plan check, on the head merged with current `idea-map` | All exit 0; CI green on the exact head |
