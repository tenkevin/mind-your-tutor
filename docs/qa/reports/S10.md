## Found during testing

1. **First click or tap on an open card near the map edge is lost (blocks F6 and U4; suggested weight: must).**
   Steps: sign in as `student-a1` with the water-cycle pack pinned, open `/` at 1280×800, click The Sun (or Plants), and the page stays on the map. Click again and it opens. Measured: the 1280×800 mouse click on `sun` and `plants` is lost the first time, while `ocean` and `evap` open on the first click. At 400×800 (touch or mouse) `sun`, `ocean` and `plants` are all lost the first time and only `evap` opens at once. At 768 px touch all open.
   Cause: pressing a card focuses it, and `onFocus` calls `reveal(n)` (`IdeaMap.tsx:279`, `:151`, `revealPoint` in `geometry.ts:54` with `margin = 90`). Any card within 90 px of the box edge makes the world pan before the button is released, so the card moves out from under the pointer. The `click` then lands on `.imap` (recorded: `pointerdown:ocean, pointerup:ocean, click:imap`) and `onClick` never runs.
   Fix direction: reveal only on keyboard focus (`:focus-visible` or no preceding pointerdown), not on pointer focus.
2. **No in-app Text size or Calm screen control (blocks U3; suggested weight: must).** `DisplaySettings` is imported only by `UdlReference.tsx`. `applyDisplay(loadDisplay())` runs in `main.tsx`, but a student can't change the setting from the map or idea page. Mount `DisplaySettings` in the student header (or document why S2 or a later task owns it, and change the S10-U3 criterion by the coordinator).
3. **Pinch zoom is inverted (blocks U4; suggested weight: must).** Likely cause (not isolated by a patch): `IdeaMap.tsx:127-130`, where the `setView` updater reads `gesture.current.pinch` when it runs, after line 130 has already set it to the new distance. The ratio is then wrong (spread zooms out, close zooms in). Reproduced with two-touch CDP events on the production build. Try capturing the previous distance in a local before calling `setView`.
4. **Layout density at 60 ideas (suggested weight: should; C3 layout, not S10 code).** With my 60-idea pack (3 categories, chained `requires`) the derived layout puts about 50 ideas on the outer ring and the cards overlap heavily at fit zoom (`shots/P1-60-ideas-unthrottled.png`), so labels are unreadable and clicks are ambiguous. S10 draws the stored layout as the criteria require, so this is a C3 or pilot-pack concern, but worth a follow-up before a pilot with a 60-idea class.
5. **Small text and clipping at fit zoom (suggested weight: should).** `fitView` floors the zoom at 0.55, so on a 400 px phone the map is clipped at the sides (Freezing, Transpiration, Water cycle are cut off) and card titles render at about 7 to 10 px; cards are 28 px high (below the 44 px target). Zoom controls overlap the Climate card. On desktop the 18-idea map renders titles at about 8 px (`shots/F9-map-desktop.png`).
6. **Idea page has no `h1` (suggested weight: could).** The map's `h1` sits in the `hidden` block, so the idea page starts at `h2` (`a11y-idea.txt`).
7. **`dev:auth` does not wire the publish routes (suggested weight: should; T8 #40 owns it).** S10-F9 says "after publishing a version to Science 6A", but the seed's published v1 has no `derived` and no class is pinned, so the student map shows "not published" until a version is pinned by hand.
