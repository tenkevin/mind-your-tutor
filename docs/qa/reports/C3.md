## Found during testing

1. **Complete-link distractors can make a true sentence when two ideas share a title** (suggest **should**). Distractors are excluded by id, but unlike false statements their finished sentence isn't checked against `allStatements`. To reproduce: 12 ideas, where `a`, `b`, `c`, `d` are titled "Water" and `e`, `f` are titled "water", with links `b>g` and `c>j` (both "leads to"). Idea `b`'s question "Water leads to …" offers options `Tj / Tk / water / Tg` with answer `Tg`, but "Water leads to Tj" is also a pack link (`c>j`). Over 30 seeds this happened 137 times; a case-variant title ("water") also showed up as a distractor 20 times. No spec rule makes titles unique, and C2 doesn't flag duplicates as far as I can see. Fix in C3 (skip a distractor whose sentence is in `allStatements`, as false statements already do), or add a duplicate-title check to C2.
2. **Some "False" statements are true in the real world** (suggest **should**, maybe a follow-up). The bar is "not any link in the pack", and that holds. But swapping in an unlinked idea can still produce something a teacher would accept, especially around the hub idea: "Oceans supplies Water cycle.", "Rain & snow feeds Water cycle.", "Clouds is a stage of Water cycle.", "Rain & snow creates Water vapour." All of these are marked False in the sample. The prototype has the same weakness. A possible mitigation is to swap only ideas at least 2 hops away (by any pack link), or not to swap in ideas that are directly linked to the other end.
3. **Grammar in generated sentences** (suggest **should**). (a) The category explanation template gives "The map says: Glaciers is a thing." and "Oceans is an object." for plural titles. (b) Swapping a singular subject into a plural relation gives "The Sun perform Transpiration.", "Freezing release Precipitation." and "Rivers soaks in as Groundwater." (b) comes from the pack's relation phrases and matches the prototype. (a) is the template's own wording; a form like "Glaciers: thing" or "Glaciers belongs to the Thing category" would avoid it.
4. **The seed is truncated to 32 bits but stored whole** (suggest **should**). `derive` stores `options.seed` but generates with `options.seed | 0`, so `seed: 2**32 + 1` gives byte-identical banks to `seed: 1` while storing a different seed. It's harmless for replay, since the stored seed reproduces the output, but either reject seeds outside int32 or store `seed | 0`.
5. **Criteria note for the coordinator.** C3-S2 calls `toString` and `hasOwnProperty` "valid slugs", but the slug pattern in `packages/contracts/src/fields.ts` rejects them because of the capitals. I tested them by passing a raw `Pack` straight to `derive`. Consider changing the example ids in `docs/qa/C3.md` to `constructor` and `valueof`.
6. **Pack-data text passes through as given** (info, for C2). The relation `…` gives "D … Dr. Who." and the relation `powers.` gives "The Sun powers. What is rain?". This is data quality for C2 to flag, not something C3 should rewrite.
