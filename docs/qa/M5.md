# QA criteria: M5 · Teacher trace views: student timeline and class heatmap

Done when: teacher views per §8.6 and D21: a per-student timeline, mastery, "may need help" with its reason, challenges and a class heatmap, for the teacher's own classes only.
Sources: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` D19–D21, D24, §8.2, §8.3, §8.6; M2, M3; T1 shell; `project/Teacher Settings.dc.html` styles.

## Functional

| Id | Weight | Check | Steps | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M5-F1 | must | Student timeline | Open a student who opened ideas, answered quizzes, asked for a hint and challenged feedback | The timeline lists exactly their §8.2 events in order, with durations as plain times; nothing D19 excludes appears |
| M5-F2 | must | Mastery and help | Same student | Mastery per idea matches M3; "may need help" shows its reason in plain words |
| M5-F3 | must | Class heatmap | A class of 25 with seeded progress | Ideas × students coloured by unlocked, passing and mastered; cells link to the student timeline |
| M5-F4 | must | Nothing D20 forbids | Inspect the views | No risk, engagement or behaviour score; no ranking of students |
| M5-F5 | must | Everything passes | Root lint, format:check, typecheck, test with DATABASE_URL (including `function_binding.test.ts`), build, plan check, on the head merged with current `idea-map` | All exit 0; CI green on the exact head |
