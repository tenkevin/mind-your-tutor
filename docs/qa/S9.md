# QA criteria: S9 · Remediation zoom

Done when: per D43 and D41, zooming in from a core idea shows the prerequisite-scope ideas and Vault standards beneath it as a separate layer. The zoom is offered when the M8 trace-back flags the student as stalled on a prerequisite. Zooming out returns to the core map.
Sources: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` D41 (trace-back goes down to the mechanism layer, not around the loop), D43 (scope; per-scope layouts), D20 (teacher-only signals: the student sees an offer, never a label), D17 (UDL), §8.4; C13 (Vault standards graph), C16 (per-scope layouts and cross-scope links), M8 (trace-back), S2 and S10 (map); `docs/decisions/2026-10-29-water-cycle-pilot-graph.md`.

Boundary:
- In scope: the zoom view and the offer to zoom.
- Out of scope: the sandbox view of extension ideas (S11, v0.3), and the teacher's diagnostic views (M9).

## Functional

|---|---|---|---|---|
| S9-F1 | must | Zoom shows what lies beneath | Zoom into Evaporation & Humidity in the pilot pack | Thermal Energy & Particle Motion appears with its standards (MS-PS1-4, MS-PS3-4), laid out from C16's prerequisite layout and joined by the cross-scope link "powers". | S9-F2 | must | Offered by the trace-back | Replay events where a student fails Evaporation and stalls on its prerequisite (M8) | The idea shows an offer to "look at what this builds on". It comes from server state, never the browser, and the student is never shown a diagnostic label (D20) |
| S9-F3 | must | Always available, never forced | A student with no trace-back flag | They can still zoom in by choice. The flag only adds the offer |
| S9-F4 | must | Lock rules hold | Zoom into an idea whose prerequisite idea is locked | Locked ideas stay locked (§8.4). Prerequisite ideas follow the same rules as core ideas |
| S9-F5 | must | Exact events (D19) | Zoom in, open a prerequisite idea, zoom out | Only §8.2 events are sent (for example `idea.opened`). Zooming itself is not recorded, unless the user adds it to D19 |
| S9-F6 | must | Everything passes | Root lint, format:check, typecheck, test with DATABASE_URL (including `function_binding.test.ts`), build and plan check, on the head merged with current `idea-map` | All exit 0; CI green on the exact head |
