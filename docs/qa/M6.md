# QA criteria: M6 · Fairness audits

Done when: per D16 and D23, demographic parity difference, disparate impact ratio (flag under 0.8) and teacher-agreement and challenge-upheld rates per question, compared across schools and districts, with groups under 10 suppressed and D24 opt-outs excluded; the sampled audit exports pseudonymous outcomes for the district to join and accepts aggregates back; Idea Map never stores race.
Sources: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` D16, D21, D23, D24, §8.6; M3; A9; F6.

## Functional

| Id | Weight | Check | Steps | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M6-F1 | must | Metrics are right | A synthetic dataset with known rates across 3 schools | Parity difference, disparate impact ratio and teacher-agreement and challenge-upheld rates match hand-computed values to 3 decimal places |
| M6-F2 | must | Flags | One school's ratio at 0.79 | Flagged; it feeds A10's drift switch for that question |
| M6-F3 | must | Small groups | A school with 9 students on a question | Suppressed everywhere, including totals that would let it be worked out by subtraction |
| M6-F4 | must | Opt-outs (D24) | Opted-out students | Excluded from every metric |
| M6-F5 | must | District sampled audit (D23) | Run an export, then import a district aggregate file | The export is a random sample of pseudonymous outcomes with no names, ids that join only with the district's key, and no race; the import accepts only aggregates (rejects row-level data) and suppresses small groups |
| M6-F6 | must | Everything passes | Root lint, format:check, typecheck, test with DATABASE_URL (including `function_binding.test.ts`), build, plan check, on the head merged with current `idea-map` | All exit 0; CI green on the exact head |
